## 1. What TecTrack does for you

Urb TecTrack is Urbeno’s e-waste **pickup lifecycle** portal. As a **Client User** you:

1. Raise a collection request for your organisation’s site.  
2. Track stages as Urbeno and the factory process the consignment.  
3. Download **Form 6** and the **Certificate of Destruction (CoD)** when available.  
4. **Close** the invoice after payment is recorded and CoD is present.  
5. Review **reports**, **Recycling Heroes**, and **sustainability** impact for **your organisation only**.

You never see other clients’ data, factory **MRN** numbers, Masters, Audit, or Compliance screens.

## 2. First login

### 2.1 Before you begin

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Portal | https://tectrack.urbeno.in |
| Browser | Latest Chrome, Edge, or Safari |
| Account email | The official address nominated by your organisation |
| Temporary password | Sent in the Urb TecTrack welcome email (not in this manual) |

### 2.2 Sign in and set your password

1. Open https://tectrack.urbeno.in.  
2. Enter your **email** and the **temporary password** from your account email.  
3. If prompted **You signed in with a temporary password**, choose a new password that meets the on-screen policy (length, upper/lower case, digit). Confirm and continue.  
4. If **Accept policies to continue** appears, open each policy link, then accept to proceed.

**Expected outcome:** You land on **Home** with a welcome heading that includes your first name. Role in profile shows **Client User**.

**Tip:** Do not share or forward the temporary-password email. If you did not receive it, contact **info@urbeno.in** — never reply with a password in clear text.

### 2.3 MFA (multi-factor authentication)

Client Users **typically do not** use MFA. Staff roles (Super Admin, Operations, Factory) do. If your Profile page has no **Two-factor authentication** card, that is expected.

### 2.4 Password later

You can change your password any time under **Profile**. Weak or reused passwords are refused by policy.

## 4. What you can and cannot do

### You can

- Raise, edit (when changes are requested), and resubmit collection requests.  
- Track stage progress on your organisation’s requests.  
- View vehicles, invoices, Form 6, and certificates when present.  
- Download Form 6 and CoD files when issued / approved for client visibility.  
- Close invoices after **payment** and **CoD** (Review & Close).  
- Run client-scoped reports and view Heroes / Sustainability.  
- Change your own password.

### You cannot

- See or create **MRN** (goods receipt) — internal to Urbeno / factory.  
- Acknowledge requests, assign vehicles, record weighment, raise invoices, or upload CoD.  
- Issue Form 6 or edit factory capacity.  
- Open another organisation’s requests (tenancy is enforced).  
- Access Masters, Audit, or Compliance.  
- Close an invoice while payment is still outstanding.

## 5. Workflow — Raise a collection request (Stage 1)

1. From **Home**, click **+ New Request** (or open **My Requests** and start a new request).  
2. Complete required fields: **site**, **pickup location**, **pickup date**, approximate **quantity** and **weight**, and at least one line item (description) or BoM as shown on the form. Optional: PO / reference and notes.  
3. Click **Submit Request**.

**Expected outcome:** You land on the request detail page with a heading like `REQ-#####`. Stage shows **Request** (awaiting Urbeno acknowledgement). Header shows your organisation, site, raised date, and raised-by email. You do **not** see Acknowledge / Assign Vehicle / Weigh / Raise Invoice.

**Tip:** The client dropdown is absent — your organisation is implied. Double-check site and location before submit; after acknowledgement, structural changes usually go through “changes requested.”

---

## 6. Workflow — Changes requested and resubmit

Sometimes Urbeno asks for a correction instead of acknowledging.

1. Open the request from **My Requests**. Look for a **Changes requested** badge and a card with Urbeno’s note.  
2. Click **Edit** (or the resubmit control). Update the fields named in the note.  
3. **Save and resubmit**.

**Expected outcome:** Message confirming the request was updated and sent back to Urbeno. Stage remains awaiting acknowledgement. You still cannot Acknowledge yourself.

**Tip:** Keep the note text handy so your resubmit answers every point in one pass.

## 7. Workflow — Track stages (2–8)

Urbeno and the factory advance the lifecycle. Refresh the request detail periodically.

| Stage (concept) | What you typically see |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| Acknowledge | Stage advances; pickup is accepted into operations |
| Assign vehicle / Load & weigh | Vehicle registration; weighment progress |
| Billing | Invoice number(s); e-way / billing details as shown |
| MRN | Processing progresses — **MRN number is hidden from you** |
| Recycling (Form 6) | Processing / Form 6 becomes available when approved for download |
| CoD upload | Certificate number and date; file download when present |

**Expected outcome:** Stage badge advances. Vehicles and invoices appear. You never see Create MRN, security-officer fields, or Upload Certificate.

**Tip:** From billing onward, each **invoice** can progress independently. The request stage reflects the least-advanced invoice.

---

## 8. Workflow — Download Form 6 and Certificate of Destruction

1. Open a request that has reached recycling / certificate stages.  
2. In the documents / invoice area, locate **Form 6** and **Certificate** (CoD) entries.  
3. Download individual files, or use **All Form 6** / equivalent bulk download if shown.

**Expected outcome:** PDF (or allowed format) downloads. Form 6 may be unavailable until admin approval — if so, wait; do not treat a missing file as a portal failure until Urbeno confirms status.

**Tip:** CoD upload also triggers an email to client users with the file attached when mail is configured. Keep both the portal download and the email copy for your records.

---

## 9. Workflow — Close after payment and CoD (Stage 9)

Only your organisation closes a normal invoice (requestor organisation).

1. Confirm a **Certificate of Destruction** exists on the invoice.  
2. Confirm **payment** has been recorded (outstanding balance cleared).  
3. Click **Review & Close**.  
4. In the modal, **Acknowledge closure**.

**Expected outcome:** Message **Invoice closed** (or equivalent). Stage shows **Closed**. Home **Completed** count increases. Sustainability figures will include this weight only after closure.

**Tip:** If **Review & Close** is missing or refused, payment is usually still outstanding or CoD is not yet uploaded. Contact Urbeno rather than forcing a close.

## 10. Home, Recycling Heroes, Sustainability

### Home

1. Open **Home**.  
2. Review tiles for open requests, completed work, and impact snapshot.  
3. Use **+ New Request** when you need another pickup.

**Expected outcome:** Tiles match your organisation only.

### Recycling Heroes

1. Open **Recycling Heroes**.  
2. Review trees / tonnage milestones for your organisation.

**Expected outcome:** Client-scoped heroes view. You do not manage Urbeno-wide planting controls.

### Sustainability

1. Open **Sustainability**.  
2. Review weight recycled, CO₂e avoided, landfill diverted, water / energy figures as presented.  
3. Open methodology (“How these numbers are built”) and download **Impact PDF** if offered.

**Expected outcome:** Figures count **closed** (certified and acknowledged) consignments only — work in progress is excluded by design.

**Tip:** Do not add “in flight” pickups into board packs; wait for closure so numbers stay defensible.

---

## 11. Reports

1. Open **Reports**.  
2. Choose a report type available to clients (e.g. Complete Request Summary, Form 6 Log, Certificate-related, Sustainability, Heroes, Device Serials — as listed in your portal).  
3. Set period filters if shown.  
4. **Export CSV** (or run on screen).

**Expected outcome:** Rows are **your organisation only**. **MRN Register** does not appear in the client report list.

**Tip:** Use period filters for finance / EHS monthly packs. Re-export after a batch of closures so CoD and sustainability columns stay current.

---

## 12. Signing out

1. Open your avatar / name menu.  
2. Choose **Sign out**.

**Expected outcome:** Login screen. Next sign-in does not re-prompt policies unless a new policy version was published.

**Tip:** Sign out on shared workstations. Do not leave a session open on a public machine.

## Appendix A — Client Read Only (brief)

Your organisation may also nominate **Client Read Only** users.

| | Client User | Client Read Only |
|--|-------------|------------------|
| View requests / reports / Heroes / Sustainability | Yes | Yes |
| Download Form 6 / CoD when available | Yes | Yes |
| Raise / edit / resubmit requests | Yes | **No** |
| Close after payment + CoD | Yes | **No** |
| New-request / operational emails | Yes | **No** (by design) |

Use Read Only for leadership, auditors, or finance who need visibility without raising work or filling the inbox.

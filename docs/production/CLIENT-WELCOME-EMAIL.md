### What we need from you

Please nominate the colleagues who should use the portal. For each person, send:

- Full name  
- Official email  
- Site / location (if relevant)  
- Function (e.g. EHS, facilities, finance, IT/asset, site in-charge, management)  
- **Access type** (see below)

#### 1. Client Users *(recommended for day-to-day owners)*

They can **raise and track** collection requests, download **Form 6** and **CoD** when available, **close** completed work after payment and CoD, and view **reports**, **Recycling Heroes**, and **sustainability** for {{ClientName}} only.

Nominate people who will own pickups end-to-end.

#### 2. Client Read Only *(optional)*

They can **view** requests and download **Form 6 / CoD**, but **cannot** raise or close requests. They also **do not** receive new-request / operational emails — ideal for leadership, auditors, or finance who need visibility without the inbox noise.

### A quick picture of TecTrack

- **Pickup lifecycle** — request → acknowledgement → vehicles → weighment → billing → recycling → certificate → closure  
- **Compliance documents** — Form 6 and Certificate of Destruction, downloadable when issued  
- **Sustainability** — impact figures built from **closed** consignments (CO₂e avoided, landfill diverted, and more)  
- **Tenant-safe** — {{ClientName}} sees only {{ClientName}}

We are pleased to invite you to participate in **User Acceptance Testing (UAT)** for **Urb TecTrack™**, Urbeno’s e-waste lifecycle portal. Your feedback as a **client (requestor)** user is essential before we proceed toward production go-live.

Please validate that you can securely raise collection requests, track progress through the operational lifecycle, review sustainability and reports for **your organisation only**, and close completed work—without seeing other clients’ data or internal Urbeno/factory controls.

### What we ask you to do

1. Complete the attached **Client UAT Results workbook (Excel)** — mark every case **Pass / Fail / N/A / Blocked** and add initials/notes.  
2. Follow the **Client Access Visual Guide (PDF)** for a step-by-step walkthrough of the portal.  
3. For every **Fail** or **Blocked** item, add a row in the **Findings** sheet (severity, summary, screenshot if possible, and steps to reproduce).  
4. Where coordination is needed (e.g. acknowledgement, invoice, certificate, payment), our team will advance the request on the Urbeno/factory side—please keep us informed of the `REQ-` number you create.  
5. Return the completed workbook by **[DD Month YYYY, time IST]** to **info@urbeno.in**, copying your Urbeno contact.
